The short version

I started working young, and those early experiences taught me responsibility, communication and how to perform under pressure. Aviation later strengthened my ability to coordinate, prioritise and respond quickly when plans change.

Breaking into marketing took persistence. I began creating content before I had an official marketing role, eventually moving into multi-brand digital marketing and later B2B technology marketing. Today, I combine that professional experience with independent client work through Social Blueprint.
