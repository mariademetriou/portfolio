Collaroid

Pet-Tech Storytelling Campaign
  • UGC
  • Storytelling
  • Pet Tech
  • Content Creation
Project

Collaroid is a smart pet identification system connecting a physical QR collar tag with a pet’s digital profile.

The campaign was designed around emotion rather than simply demonstrating the technology.

The concept

Pets bring happiness, companionship and familiarity into everyday life. Losing one can create panic and uncertainty.

The creative idea focused on that emotional contrast and positioned Collaroid as a tool that can help create reassurance and connection when a pet goes missing.
