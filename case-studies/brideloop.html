A high-level look at the growth thinking and campaign creative behind a two-sided bridal marketplace.

The project

BrideLoop is a startup marketplace created specifically for buying and reselling pre-owned wedding dresses.

The business needs both sellers bringing quality dresses to the platform and buyers discovering relevant dresses and contacting sellers.

The challenge

A marketplace cannot grow effectively by focusing on only one audience.

Sellers

More quality listings.

Give dress owners a clear reason to list and a simple path to act.

↔
Buyers

More relevant discovery.

Help future brides find options that match their style and budget.

My role
Paid Media StrategyMeta AdsCreative StrategyVideo ConceptsScriptwritingVideo CreationCampaign Testing
The approach

Two separate campaign directions were created for sellers and buyers, each built around their different motivations.

Seller messaging focused on giving an unused wedding dress another opportunity. Buyer messaging focused on discovering more relevant options within a preferred style and budget. Creative variations tested different opening hooks while keeping the core message consistent.
